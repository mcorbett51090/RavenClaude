# Tolerances

## Contents

- [Quantity](#quantity)
- [Price](#price)
- [Freight](#freight)

## Quantity
Received quantity below ordered is a short-ship, not a discrepancy, unless the invoice
bills the ordered amount.

## Price
Any unit-price difference from the purchase order is a discrepancy regardless of size.
Small differences are the ones that recur.

## Freight
Freight absent from the purchase order but present on the invoice is a discrepancy unless
the supplier agreement lists it.
