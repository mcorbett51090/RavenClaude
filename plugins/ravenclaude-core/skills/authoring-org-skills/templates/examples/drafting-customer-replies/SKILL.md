# drafting-customer-replies

Produces an editable first draft that cites the account's own facts. The draft is always
reviewed by a person before it is sent.

## Not for

- Legal or contractual commitments — route to the contracts owner.
- Security-incident notifications — those follow the incident runbook, not this.
- Anything the account record does not answer. Say so in the draft instead of guessing.

## Procedure

1. Read the inbound message and classify it against the categories in
   [the category map](reference/categories.md).
2. Pull the account record: plan, renewal date, open tickets, recent invoices.
3. Draft the reply using the tone for that audience in
   [the tone guide](reference/tone.md).
4. Cite the specific fact behind every claim about the account. Where the record is
   silent, write what is missing rather than inferring it.
5. Hand the draft to a person. Do not send.

## Escalation

A refund over the approval threshold, a churn signal, or a legal reference goes to the
account owner with the draft attached and a one-line reason.
