# Categories

## Contents

- [Billing](#billing)
- [Renewal](#renewal)
- [Outage](#outage)

## Billing
A charge the customer does not recognise, a duplicate charge, or a plan-change proration.
Pull the last three invoices before drafting.

## Renewal
A renewal inside 60 days, an upgrade question, or a cancellation notice. Pull the renewal
date and any open tickets.

## Outage
A report of unavailability. Confirm against the status record before conceding an outage.
